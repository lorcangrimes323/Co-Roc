Co-Roc logo concepts

Five deterministic SVG systems. Every mark is built from native vector geometry and editable markup; there are no generated or raster assets.

  1. 01Orbit DatumMost balanced. A product-grade mark connecting trajectory and controlled geometry.
  2. 02Section MarkMost aerospace-specific. Reads like a vehicle section and interface drawing.
  3. 03Controlled MonogramMost technical. Strong at small sizes and suitable for application icons.
  4. 04Trace LineMost software-led. Expresses the history of a configuration through controlled states.
  5. 05Linked ReleaseMost collaborative. Connects team records, approval and release status.
